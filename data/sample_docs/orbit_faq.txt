Orbit T2 Smart Thermostat - Frequently Asked Questions
(Lumen Home is a fictional company. Sample data for the MultiSource RAG Chatbot.)

Q: Does the Orbit T2 work with a heat pump?
A: Yes. During setup, choose "Heat pump" as the system type. If your heat pump has an O/B
reversing valve wire, connect it to the O/B terminal and pick the correct energize mode in
Settings > Equipment.

Q: Can I control the thermostat without the internet?
A: Yes. The touch screen works without Wi-Fi. You lose remote control from the app, Away mode
and energy reports until the connection returns. Your schedule keeps running.

Q: How many thermostats can one account have?
A: Up to 20 thermostats across up to 5 homes on one Lumen Home account.

Q: Can I share control with my family?
A: Yes. In the app, open Account > Household and invite people by email. Invited members can
change the temperature and schedules but cannot remove the device or delete data.

Q: Why does the display turn off?
A: To save energy, the screen dims after 30 seconds and turns off after 2 minutes. It wakes up
when you walk past it, thanks to the motion sensor. You can change this in Settings > Display.

Q: What is the temperature accuracy?
A: The built-in sensor is accurate to plus or minus 0.5 degrees Celsius. You can add up to six
wireless room sensors to average temperatures across rooms.

Q: Does the Orbit T2 work with voice assistants?
A: Yes, it works with the major voice assistants through the Lumen Home app integrations page.

Q: How do I reset the thermostat to factory settings?
A: Go to Settings > Reset > Factory reset on the device and confirm twice. This removes the
thermostat from your account and erases its schedules. Wiring settings are also cleared.
